# FUTURE 20 - Resilience and Packaging

Status: BACKLOG

Implement only after the MVP stop gate is explicitly lifted.

Validation targets:

- Explorer restart;
- monitor hotplug;
- resolution switch;
- DPI change;
- sleep/wake;
- lock/unlock;
- RDP;
- fullscreen apps;
- mixed refresh rates;
- abrupt termination;
- graphics device loss.

Packaging targets:

- portable build;
- installer;
- clean uninstall;
- no service;
- no driver;
- no admin requirement for normal operation.
