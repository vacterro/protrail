# FUTURE 12 - Optional Particle Layer

Status: BACKLOG

Implement only after the MVP stop gate is explicitly lifted.

Add an optional particle emitter around movement.

Initial shapes may include:

- triangle;
- circle;
- square.

Settings:

- amount;
- lifetime;
- spread;
- size;
- velocity;
- opacity;
- shape.

Default must remain OFF.

Particles must be a separate effect/layer, not baked into the trail algorithm.
